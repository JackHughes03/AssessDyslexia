Our Assessment Process

  1. 1

    Gathering Information

    My process for assessing is gathering information from home and school so there is a clear picture of the learner's strengths and difficulties.

  2. 2

    Home-Based Assessment

    The assessment would take place in the individual's home so parents can stay on site and the pupil is in a secure and comfortable environment.

    Assessment Areas:

    • • Language, verbal and visual ability
    • • Reading and spelling
    • • Memory and processing information
    • • Mathematics (if highlighted as difficulty)

    Assessment Details:

    • • Approximately three hours duration
    • • Breaks built in
    • • Parents stay on site
    • • Secure and comfortable environment
  3. 3

    Report Writing

    The report would be written and shared within three weeks.

  4. 4

    Follow-up Meeting

    I would follow the report up with a meeting to explain the results and answer any questions.
